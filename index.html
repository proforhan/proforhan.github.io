01 About

Orhan Erdem is an economist and Clinical Associate Professor at the University of North Texas. His work combines artificial intelligence, data science, behavioral economics, and applied econometrics to turn complex evidence into practical insights.

Before joining UNT, he directed the Research Department at Borsa Istanbul and held academic appointments in the United States and Turkey, including at the University of Texas at Dallas, Texas A&M University-Commerce, and Rockford University.
