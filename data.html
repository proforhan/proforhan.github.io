What this measures. The Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI) estimates the typical value of homes in the middle third of the market (33rd–67th percentile), for single-family homes and condos combined. It is smoothed and seasonally adjusted. It is not an average sale price or a listing price. Dallas, Plano and Frisco are city boundaries; “United States” is the national index.

Growth view. Each line is rescaled to 100 at the start of the chosen time range, so you can compare percentage growth directly.

Updates. Zillow publishes new figures around the middle of each month for the previous month. A scheduled job on this site downloads them and refreshes this page.
