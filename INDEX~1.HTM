01 About

Orhan Erdem is an economist and Clinical Associate Professor at the University of North Texas. His work combines artificial intelligence, data science, behavioral economics, and applied econometrics to turn complex evidence into practical insights.

Before joining UNT, he directed the Research Department at Borsa Istanbul and held academic appointments in the United States and Turkey, including at the University of Texas at Dallas, Texas A&M University-Commerce, and Rockford University.

He is also Founder & CEO of Datarium, an AI consulting and product studio at the intersection of finance and economics, and builds StockPik, an AI-assisted stock research app. Through Erdem Consulting LLC, he has advised financial institutions including ING Bank and Yapı Kredi Bank on credit-risk modeling and consumer-finance analytics.

The Socioeconomic Impact of Pandemics

How COVID-19 Reshaped the World

Palgrave Macmillan · 2026

A narrative exploration of how the COVID-19 pandemic transformed societies, economies, and institutions — and what history can teach us about future crises.

“A terrific book that weaves history, literature, and modern analysis into a clear and compelling narrative. Drawing on voices from Camus, Vonnegut, and Marcus Aurelius, alongside vivid storytelling such as the Dr. Karikó case, it makes sense of one of the most disorienting crises of our time without losing nuance or humanity. Above all, it captures the central truths of crisis: that the vulnerable suffer most; fear erodes logic; and democracy itself is at risk. A thoughtful, heterodox work with a final line that lingers long after the last page.” — Robert Maranto, University of Arkansas
